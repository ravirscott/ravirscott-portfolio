Ravish Kumar
aka Ravir Scott

Born and raised in Sonma village, Begusarai, Bihar — Ravir Scott is an independent Artist, Developer, and Author who moves between emotional rap, hip-hop, cinematic storytelling, and modern technology.

Entirely self-taught and self-produced, he has built a body of work spanning music, writing, AI tools, web development, and cybersecurity awareness — all under his own name, on his own terms.

He operates with a singular focus: create meaningful music and build powerful technology tools that last.

02

Journey

Chapter 01 — Early Life
The Seed
Started creative writing and discovered a deep interest in music at a young age in Sonma village. The curiosity that would define everything began here.
Chapter 02 — Learning Phase
Self-Teaching
Began self-learning coding, technology, and AI tools. No formal institution — pure curiosity, late nights, and relentless building.
Chapter 03 — Music Journey
Independent Artist
Entered independent music creation and distribution. Released emotional rap and cinematic compositions, including Lone Yet Loyal. Self-produced from start to finish.
Chapter 04 — Developer Path
Builder
Built technical projects across web, cybersecurity awareness, and AI. Published tech content on Dev.to, Hashnode, and Medium under the Ravir Scott name.
Chapter 05 — Present
Active & Expanding
Actively creating as Artist & Developer. Working on new music, open-source tools, digital archives, and growing a community across platforms.
Chapter 06 — Future
The Vision
To create music that moves people and technology tools that empower them. Autonomous, consistent, meaningful — that is the goal.
